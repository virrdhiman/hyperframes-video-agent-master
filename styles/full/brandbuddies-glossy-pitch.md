# Full style — BrandBuddies "Glossy Pitch"

Warm-room talking-head **pitch/explainer** reel with glossy 3D-extruded blue keywords, white
blueprint slides, phone-mockup portfolio cards and an iMessage-style CTA. Decoded from the
reference reel (instagram.com/p/DZQRj33AC3r · 1080×1920 · 30fps · ~35.9s · @BrandBuddies Studios).
Reproduce the SPEC — never clone a frame-for-frame. Reference frames: `~/workspace/workspace/style_study/ig_DZQRj33/`
(sheet.jpg contact-grid + frames/f_*.jpg).

## When to use
Service / agency / SaaS PITCH shorts where a person talks to camera and "sells" an offering:
problem → solution → proof (portfolio) → CTA. Premium-but-relatable. Great when you want a
designed, "we make content for a living" meta vibe (selection handles, mockup cards) rather than
the punchy number-motif of webveda.

## Palette → (new) glossy-blue + warm-room
- **Signature glossy blue keyword:** light top `#71D4FE` → mid `#3FA9F5`, dark bevel/extrude
  `#2D3C53`, with a soft drop shadow. This gradient is the whole brand — reused for keywords,
  "Studios", and card header words.
- Warm room set: amber lamp glow `#E8A24C`, deep green velvet chair `~#0E2A22`, navy suit
  `~#101725`, beige wall with a window-grid light gobo. Subject sharp, background softly blurred.
- White slides: pure `#FFFFFF`, faint blueprint grid `~#E8ECF2`, bold near-black headline `#111`.
- Red used sparingly as a label accent (e.g. "SHOOT", tie on the badge icon) `#FF3B30`.

## Typography
Heavy **rounded geometric sans, often italic** (Montserrat/Poppins Black or similar). Two weights:
- Caption context line — small, white, **italic, sentence case** (`if you are a`).
- Hero keyword — huge, **ALL-CAPS bold italic, glossy-blue 3D extrude** (`BRAND OWNER`).
- Slide headlines — bold black, sometimes with a grid/letterpress texture (`How to shoot`).

## Composition (1080×1920)
- Talking head CENTERED, head upper-center, cozy real-room bokeh behind.
- **FACE ZONE clear (~y600–1180).** Hard rule.
- Captions + CTA sit LOWER-CENTER over the **suit/chest** (~y1240–1400) — note: this look
  intentionally lets text touch the BODY, never the FACE (differs from webveda's no-body rule,
  same face-safe principle).
- Badges + floating emoji live in the TOP HEADROOM (~y100–620).
- White slides + portfolio cards take the WHOLE frame (full-bleed cutaways, no talking head).

## Signature techniques (what makes this look)
1. **Glossy 3D-extruded blue keyword** — the hero device. Light-cyan top, blue body, dark bevel,
   drop shadow; ALL-CAPS bold italic. Used for caption keywords, "Studios", card headers.
2. **Two-tier captions** — tiny white italic context line stacked over the giant glossy keyword.
3. **Circular white badge drop-ins** with flat **long-shadow** icons (businessman-in-armchair, etc.).
4. **White blueprint slides** — full-white, faint grid, bold textured black headline, dashed-circle
   line icon. The "explainer / teaching" beats.
5. **Design-software selection-handle motif** — corner anchor squares around text, as if it's
   selected in Figma/Canva. Meta cue: "we design content."
6. **Phone-mockup portfolio cards** — rounded-rect cards with heavy drop shadow showing real
   vertical clips; two-tone grey+blue header label. The social proof.
7. **Floating Apple-style 3D emoji** (glowing "?") above the head on question beats.
8. **iMessage / sticker CTA** — white rounded pill + blue rounded-square send button (black
   up-arrow) + small square brand sticker beside it.
9. **Transitions** — white-flash page-fold (folded corner) into slides; vertical-blind line wipe
   between beats.
10. **Cozy warm real-room set** (amber lamp, green velvet chair, plant, window-gobo) — premium yet
    relatable, NOT a sterile studio. Subject sharp, bg blurred.

## Overlap rules
MAY: caption/CTA over the suit & chest · badge over top headroom · floating emoji over headroom ·
slide/card take the whole frame.
MUST NOT: anything over the FACE zone (~y600–1180) · two hero blocks competing · text bleeding to
the extreme edge (keep ~80–105px margins).
