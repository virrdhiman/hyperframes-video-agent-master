CURATED MOTION-DESIGN / ART-DIRECTION TEACHING VIDEOS
(for the scene-redesign effort — fixing flat vector-in-void graphics)

Tier A — downloaded + frame-sampled into grids/ (richest, watch first)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. "10 Underrated Motion Design Techniques I LOVE" — Ben Marriott
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a7Nyzqy9_I0
   WHY: 15 seconds of finished animation reverse-engineered into 10 concrete,
   codifiable techniques: gate-weave wiggle(12,3), posterize-time to 12fps for a
   handmade feel, simple-choker edge rounding, arcing splash motion, eye-trace,
   contrast-driven hierarchy, "show the result of an action." The single best
   source of exact values we can paste into Remotion.

2. "Designers Dissect The Graphic Design of DUNE" — Ben Marriott + Elliot
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uPefvc-sHhU
   WHY: A 25-min running critique of ~40 posters/covers that names our exact
   failure modes out loud — "so little depth," "feels so flat," "generic sans
   serif," "minimalist that fails" — and what the EXPENSIVE versions do instead
   (scale, gradient palettes, drop shadows, one accent color per item,
   repeated-shape systems). The clearest map of cheap-vs-premium signals.

3. "Advanced Lighting in After Effects" — Ben Marriott
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rdi0bPWqYyI
   WHY: The literal recipe for making a flat vector sit inside a world:
   desaturate to integrate, color-match with curves, 2px Gaussian blur to kill
   vector crispness, add HLS grain/noise to flat fills, duplicate-layer soft
   shadows with feathering + reduced opacity. Directly attacks "flat shape
   floating in void."

Tier B — transcript-reviewed, cited in the principles doc (no grid)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. "The ONLY Graphic Design Video On Space You Need To See!" — DesignCourse (Gareth David)
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3QJKOWTRuE4
   WHY: The cleanest taxonomy of space we found: macro vs micro white space,
   passive vs active white space, proximity grouping, and "negative space is not
   white space." Turns "empty void" into "intentional negative space" with rules.

5. "Designers Dissect the Most Unexpected Rebrands of 2023" — Ben Marriott + Linus Boman
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3cg8Cy6AKJg
   WHY: Names the "re-blanding" trend (stripping all character to a generic sans
   = the cheap default) and the construction-grid/golden-ratio satire. A
   cautionary catalogue of what reads as lazy/template.

6. "Why Your Animation Looks Bad (Fix Floaty Movements...)" — Robin (animator)
   https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-FHb6XFHyT4
   WHY: Pose-to-pose planning, slow-in/slow-out, anticipation->action->reaction,
   "no constant or purposeless movement," "white space = focus." Transferable to
   motion-graphic choreography even though framed for character animation.
